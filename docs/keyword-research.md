## Research method and limits

Live web searches examined `wireless adb mac macos QR code pairing without android studio`, `"Android Connect" "macOS" adb`, and `adb no route to host mac local network wireless debugging`. The results contain official Android documentation, competing open-source tools and error-specific troubleshooting. These are qualitative intent signals, not measured demand or Google position reports.

OpenSEO research_keywords was requested for `wireless adb`, `adb mac` and `android wireless debugging`; all failed with INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS. A fixed 11-keyword metrics request also failed for insufficient credits. Volume, keyword difficulty and CPC are unknown for every term below. No paid tracking was enabled, no keywords saved, and no rankings or traffic are promised.

## Positioning and metadata

Product name: Android Connect.
Repository slug: android-connect.
README / homepage title: Android Connect — Wireless ADB & QR Pairing for macOS.
GitHub description: Native macOS menu bar app for wireless ADB and Android QR pairing. Pair over Wi-Fi without opening Android Studio. SwiftUI, Bonjour discovery, start at login. MIT.
Homepage description: Open-source wireless ADB and QR pairing for macOS. Connect Android from your Mac menu bar without opening Android Studio.

The name retains the installed app's identity. The descriptive subtitle disambiguates it from Android file-transfer and phone-linking products. Do not target unsupported screen mirroring, file transfer, iOS, Windows, Android below 11, or guaranteed background reconnection.

## Launch copy draft

Title: Android Connect: wireless ADB QR pairing from your Mac menu bar (MIT)

I built Android Connect, a small native macOS menu bar app for Android wireless debugging. Scan a QR from Android's Wireless debugging settings, use a six-digit pairing code, or connect by IP. It uses your existing ADB installation, supports Bonjour discovery and optional startup at login, and includes guidance for the macOS Local Network permission problem. Requires macOS 13+ and Android 11+. The first release is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Source, builds and limitations: https://github.com/GoharAli16/android-connect
